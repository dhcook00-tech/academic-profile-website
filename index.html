Research Interests

Understanding the multi-length scale origins of mechanical properties, with an emphasis on fracture and fatigue of advanced structural materials in extreme temperature, chemical, and radiation environments. Studying the nanoscale origins of plastic deformation with an emphasis on dislocation dynamics, twinning, phase transformations, and kink band formation.

The development of of stronger, lighter, tougher, and more environmentally resistant structural materials is imperative for the design of safety-critical components in fission and fusion reactors, as well as advanced propulsion systems. Unfortunately, there exist inherent trade-offs in all these properties. Our goal is to understand ways in which they can be overcome or even cheated to design better materials.
